Brief Bio

Ken

Ken Carson is a professional rapper and songwriter. He is known for his energetic performances and unique style, which blends elements of hip-hop, punk, and electronic music.
He performs a high-energy, aggressive subgenre of hip-hop known as rage rap, featuring distorted synths, heavy bass, and a chaotic, punk-rock-inspired aesthetic.

Ken Carson has released several albums and singles, and has performed at numerous music festivals and events. He has a dedicated fan base and is known for his engaging live shows.
